About This Project

This tool analyzes the IMDB collaboration graph — a network where every node is an actor or director and every edge represents co-appearing in at least one film or TV show. Edge weight is the number of shared titles, so two frequent co-stars have a heavier edge than two people who worked together only once.

Centrality Rankings

Degree = number of distinct collaborators (fast).
Eigenvector = rewards having well-connected collaborators (power iteration, may be slow on large graphs).
Katz = like eigenvector but with a baseline score for every node (power iteration, may be slow).

⚠ Eigenvector / Katz centrality runs power iteration over the full graph. This may take 30 – 120 s on large datasets. Hang tight…

Triadic Closure

An open triad exists when person A collaborated with B and C, but B and C have never worked together. A strong open triad requires both A–B and A–C edges to have weight ≥ 2 (multiple shared titles).
